TRANSPARENCY & DATA USE

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

CurioPulse Bot is a personal, owner-operated automation that creates and uploads educational videos to the CurioPulse YouTube channel. This policy describes the workflow and this informational website.

Google and YouTube access

Only the channel owner signs in through Google's OAuth authorization process. CurioPulse Bot requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload to upload generated videos and related titles, descriptions and metadata to the owner's YouTube channel. It does not ask visitors to sign in or connect their accounts, and it does not request access to Gmail or Google Drive.

Use of information

OAuth authorization is used only to complete authorized YouTube uploads. The application does not sell Google user data, use it to target advertising, or provide visitor account access.

Storage and security

OAuth client details and refresh tokens are kept in encrypted GitHub Actions repository secrets in a private automation repository, not on this public website. Generated videos and non-sensitive metadata may be retained as workflow artifacts for approximately seven days. Uploaded videos are stored and governed by YouTube's policies. Tokens remain configured until the channel owner removes or rotates them.

Third-party services

GitHub hosts the automation and this website. Google and YouTube provide OAuth authorization and video uploading. Public materials, such as Google Trends and Wikipedia, may be used for topic research. Optional AI text scripting may send public topics and source excerpts to a third-party text-generation provider; it is not intended to send viewers' personal data.

Website visitors

This site has no login forms, comments, analytics scripts, or site-controlled tracking cookies. GitHub Pages may process technical access information under GitHub's own policies when delivering web pages.

Sharing, choices and deletion

The workflow shares uploaded video data with YouTube to perform the owner's request; service providers process the information necessary to operate their services. The channel owner may revoke permission at Google Account connections, delete GitHub repository secrets, or remove uploaded videos in YouTube Studio. Revoking access prevents further authorized uploads until the owner grants permission again.

Questions and changes

For project inquiries, visit the project owner's public GitHub profile or the CurioPulse channel. We will update this notice if the project's data practices change.

CurioPulse Bot is for the owner's YouTube channel only. This is not a service for the public to link their own Google accounts.
